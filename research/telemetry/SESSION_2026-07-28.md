## Continuation: active RTC producer race

A deeper trace of the actual RTC implementation invalidated one earlier assumption:
calling `pause()` before `reset()` does not prove quiescence. Pause only clears the
producer event, so an inference that already passed that check can finish after reset and
merge its pre-trigger chunk into the empty queue. That is precisely the stale-action race
the recovery boundary must exclude.

Added `RecoverySafeActionQueue` and `RecoverySafeRTCInferenceEngine` in the research tree.
Every inference cursor now carries a queue generation. Suspend, reset, and resume advance
that generation, and merge rejects an old cursor atomically. A deterministic threaded test
starts a producer before recovery, allows it to finish after resume, and verifies its merge
is rejected and the queue stays empty. Upstream source remains untouched.

Also moved D0 reseeding into `RolloutRecoveryLifecycle.reinvoke()`: the fresh normalized
start observation is published, validated, passed to the scorer, and only then may policy
production resume. Missing start state fails before resume. The focused recovery subset
contains 26 passing tests, including the active producer race and normalized detector
reset; the later composed-loop tests brought the combined telemetry suite to 67 at that
stage.

This closes the isolated RTC race primitive, not the whole live acceptance gate. The
guarded engine still needs selection by the future concrete recovery-enabled strategy,
