# CoRL 2026 workshop tracker

**Checked:** 28 July 2026 against official conference/workshop pages.

CoRL workshops are on **9 November 2026** at JW Marriott Austin. The conference explicitly
welcomes contributed papers with preliminary and in-progress work, but each accepted
workshop sets its own paper rules and deadline.

## Best current targets

1. **Continually Self-Improving Robots** — strongest fit. Its call explicitly includes
   failure discovery, automatic reset systems, safe exploration, and reliable evaluation
   environments. It has an OpenReview call but currently publishes no submission date.
   <https://csircorl.github.io/website/>
2. **Human-Centered Robot Learning and Interaction** — secondary fit for reliable autonomy,
   evaluation beyond task success, safety, and recovery. It states non-archival extended
   abstracts up to four pages, but its rendered timeline still contains placeholders rather
   than a calendar date. <https://hc-robot-learning.github.io/>
3. **Memory for Robot Foundation Models** — fallback if the paper emphasizes remembered
   failures/action traces and policy recovery. It explicitly welcomes work-in-progress,
   negative/ablation results, protocols, datasets, and system studies; deadline is `TBA`.
   <https://corl2026-memory.github.io/>

Official conference workshop guidance and event date:
<https://2026.corl.org/contributions/call-for-workshops>

## Watch rule

Check the three pages and their OpenReview groups every Monday. Record the first actual
deadline, timezone, page revision date, format/page limit, archival status, and notification
date here. Do not plan against the proposal’s old “late September / early October” estimate:
as of this check, no authoritative target-workshop deadline is published.

Prepare a four-page non-archival draft path first because it fits the two most relevant
calls. The minimum submission package is the frozen taxonomy, Week 1 signatures, D0/D0r
results including the failed B hold-out, recovery/alignment infrastructure, limitations,
and a clear statement that corpus/closed-loop results are pending.
