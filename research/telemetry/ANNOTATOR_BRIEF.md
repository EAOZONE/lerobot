# Independent annotator brief and outreach template

**Need:** one person available during Week 9 to label a blinded, stratified 15% subset.

The annotator receives `LABELING_GUIDE.md`, the annotation tool/schema, synchronized wrist
and overhead video, and telemetry plots. They do not receive the first annotator’s labels,
detector outputs, or threshold decisions. Expected outputs are fine/collapsed class,
success, onset uncertainty interval, unrecoverable uncertainty interval, and notes for
ambiguous cases.

The subset will be stratified by task, outcome, collapsed class, and session after the
corpus split is frozen. Agreement reporting includes Cohen’s kappa for class labels and
exact/±1/±3/±5-frame onset agreement plus interval overlap. Budget time for one guide
orientation, independent labeling, and one adjudication meeting; adjudication does not
overwrite the independently reported agreement.

## Outreach text

> I’m running an SO-101 robot-failure study and need an independent second annotator for a
> blinded 15% subset during the project’s Week 9. The task is to review two synchronized
> camera views using a frozen guide, classify rollout failures, and mark uncertainty
> intervals around failure onset and the last recoverable frame. You would not see my
> labels or detector predictions. Can you commit to an orientation, the labeling block,
> and a short adjudication meeting during that week? I’ll provide the guide, examples,
> annotation schema, and an estimate of clips/hours once the corpus size is final.

Record the contacted person, date, response, availability window, conflicts of interest,
and final commitment in the dated session log. The gate is not complete until a person has
accepted; this document only removes ambiguity from the request.
